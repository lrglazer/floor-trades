About this data. Members of Congress must report trades over $1,000 within 45 days under the STOCK Act of 2012. Disclosures give dollar ranges, not exact amounts, so volumes here use range midpoints. Many trades are made by a spouse or joint account. Disclosure is required by law and does not imply wrongdoing. This is a curated sample of 2026 filings, not every trade, and it is not investment advice.
